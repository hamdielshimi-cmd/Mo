02 | Why Egypt?

Egypt continues to be one of the Middle East's most culturally influential destinations, where music, creativity and community naturally intersect.

It attracts local audiences, regional travelers, expatriates and international visitors, making it an ideal place for brands and collectives looking to expand beyond their home market.

Introducing LYMM to Egypt

This collaboration creates the opportunity to establish a meaningful presence in Egypt while introducing its artists, identity and community to a new audience through an authentic local platform.

03 | Why Fayoum?

A Destination Worth Traveling For

Located just outside Cairo, Fayoum is one of Egypt's most remarkable destinations.

Its unique combination of nature, culture and heritage transforms the weekend into a complete destination experience rather than simply another music event.

Only a short drive from Cairo, it offers a combination of experiences found nowhere else in the country:

  • Desert landscapes, ancient sightseeing and towering sand dunes
  • Lakes and waterfalls
  • Valley of the Whales
  • Countryside and palm groves
  • Stargazing
  • Horseback riding and outdoor adventures
  • Authentic Tunis Village
  • Traditional pottery workshops
  • Local crafts and artisans
  • Authentic farm to table Egyptian cuisine
  • A slower and more immersive rhythm of life

People don't only come for the music.
They come for the experience.

11 | Partnership Framework

The objective of this collaboration is to establish LYMM's presence in Egypt through a carefully curated destination showcase while creating long term value for both collectives.

To bring this collaboration to life, LYMM joins N*RDS as the official showcase partner through a Partnership Commitment ranging from USD 8,000 to 10,000, establishing the foundation required to confidently deliver the collaboration from concept to completion.

The final commitment will be confirmed based on the agreed scope of the collaboration.

This commitment enables the collaboration in its entirety, supporting every aspect outlined throughout this proposal, from artistic integration and destination experience to production, hospitality, marketing, content creation and local execution. It establishes a solid foundation before ticket sales begin, allowing both teams to focus on creating an exceptional experience while aligning around a shared vision for the project.

As outlined throughout this proposal, DAL Experiences forms part of the overall N*RDS collaboration, enriching the wider festival experience. All related arrangements are incorporated within the N*RDS share.
